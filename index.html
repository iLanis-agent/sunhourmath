Boundary: illustrative arithmetic only Sun Hour Math keeps labeled-norm arithmetic only. Hour bands and shadow multipliers are labeled norms, not measurements of your yard, your season or your plants - real sun depends on your latitude, your trees and your weather. Watch your own garden for a week, follow the plant tag over anything this page says, and ask a local nursery about your microclimate.

The label truth

Six hours of direct sun reads full sun honestly; 5.5 reads part sun. The band edges were never vibes, and a tomato asking 8 hours in a bed offering 5.5 runs 2.5 hours short honestly.

The shadow truth

A 6-foot fence throws a 2.4-foot shadow in summer and a 12.0-foot shadow in winter. The garden you planned in July was always going to move in November.

The stolen sun truth

A bed starting 4 feet from the fence and running 6 feet deep spends all 6.0 feet inside that winter shadow - or sits fully clear of it. Distance is the whole decision.
